    Tips for accurate counts
    • One tablet type per photo.
    • A single layer only: no tablets on top of others.
    • A plain background that contrasts with the tablets (dark cloth for white tablets, white paper for coloured ones).
    • Hold the phone straight above, about 20–30 cm away.
    • No flash, and avoid glare from lamps or windows.
    TabCount is a counting aid. Always verify counts before dispensing.
